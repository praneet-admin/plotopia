# Audience models

Seated audience figures are baked from:

- **Universal Base Characters (Standard)** by Quaternius — https://quaternius.com — CC0 1.0 Universal
- **Universal Animation Library (Standard)** by Quaternius — `Sitting_Idle_Loop`, `Sitting_Talking_Loop` — CC0 1.0 Universal

`people-hi.*` are the original meshes posed and quantised; `people-lo.*` are simplified to about a fifth of the triangles for phones. Regenerate with `tools/bake.mjs` (see README).
